Your sleep position has a bigger effect on mattress fit than almost any other factor. Here's what the research says about back, side, and stomach sleeping.

## Side Sleepers

Side sleepers generally benefit from a softer surface that cushions the shoulders and hips, keeping the spine in a neutral line.

## Back Sleepers

Back sleepers tend to do best on a medium-firm surface that supports the lower back without letting the hips sink too far.

## Stomach Sleepers

Stomach sleepers usually need a firmer mattress to prevent the midsection from sinking and straining the lower back.

## Takeaway

There's no single "best" mattress — the right firmness depends on how you sleep, your body weight, and any existing pain points.
