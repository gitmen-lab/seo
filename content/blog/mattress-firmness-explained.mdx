Mattress firmness is usually rated on a scale from 1 (very soft) to 10 (very firm). Most adults land somewhere between 4 and 7.

## Why Firmness Matters

Firmness affects spinal alignment, pressure relief, and how easily you can move on the surface.

## Matching Firmness to Body Weight

Heavier sleepers typically need a firmer base to avoid excessive sinkage, while lighter sleepers often prefer a softer top layer for pressure relief.

## Our Recommendation

When in doubt, a medium-firm hybrid mattress (around 6 on the firmness scale) works well for most combination sleepers.
